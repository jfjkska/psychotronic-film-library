**Psychotronic Film Library** is Darren Evanson's shelf of the films that never made it
to the multiplex: Italian giallo, eurosleaze, grindhouse exploitation, video-nasty
horror, mondo, softcore oddities, cannibal pictures, poliziotteschi and anything else
with a lurid poster and a lounge soundtrack.

Every review is a short, straight account of what actually happens in the film and
whether it is worth your evening. Ratings are out of five eyes. Five means drop
everything. One means you have been warned.

Posters and stills belong to their studios and distributors and are shown here for
review and comment. If you own one and would rather it was not here, [get in touch]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }})
and it will come down.
