    Also known as…
    How we connect the dots

    Shared credits: drawn from the director, cast and music fields of every published review. Additional library notes supply David McGillivray’s writing credits and Candace Glendenning in The Flesh and Blood Show. These are selected credits, not complete filmographies.

    Names: accents and apostrophe variants are normalised, with an explicit list of known pseudonyms. A band is not silently equated with a member. A music-library credit is a shared credit, not proof of the same composer or recording.

    Detours: curated links through outside titles, television, cycles and companies. Newly researched additions include source links. Earlier entries are labelled “Library research notes”. Themes, family relationships and our double bills do not create shortcuts in the route finder.

    Routes: the fewest connecting steps in the selected network. A shared credit takes one step; entering and leaving an outside detour takes two. Ties are resolved consistently. No route means no link in this dataset, not no link in cinema history.

    Keeping up: film counts, credit links and career lines are rebuilt from the reviews. The outside shelf and programme notes are editorial research. A release year here follows the review metadata.
