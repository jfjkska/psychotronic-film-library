A vintage slice of blaxploitation from 1972. William Marshall is the cursed
African prince dubbed Blacula by the original Count Dracula, then encased in a
coffin in the Count's Transylvanian castle. He is shipped to the States by "two
faggot interior decorators", in the film's own words, who really should have
checked the contents of the coffin beforehand.

Played straight by all involved, it belongs to the spate of modern vampire
films such as Count Yorga and Kolchak: The Night Stalker which, like Hammer's
films, relocated the vampire story to the present day. It must have done
sufficiently well at the box office to get William Marshall to don the cape,
sideburns and teeth one more time in Scream, Blacula, Scream. Hot on its heels
came Blackenstein. They really knew how to exploit those black audiences in
the 70s looking for something outside the Hollywood staple.
