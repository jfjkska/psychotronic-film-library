*Warning: contains plot spoilers. Only watch if you are a fan of dreamlike,
surreal French horror, and then read on.*

The Iron Rose is a 1973 arthouse horror by French auteur Jean Rollin. Rollin
was famous for his erotic vampire films, but this is his first not to feature
them.

Francoise Pascal, of Mind Your Language fame, is courted by Hugues Quester
when they meet at a wedding reception. They go on a first date, a bike ride,
and stop at a cemetery. They enter a crypt and make out, only emerging when it
is dark. They find themselves lost in the cemetery and a personality change
takes over them both as they experience fear and panic. Pascal, aka The Girl,
locks the Boy in the crypt when he returns to retrieve his watch. She dances
round the cemetery as he suffocates. When daylight arrives she descends into
the crypt, closing the shutters and embracing death.

This is only my third Rollin film I can recall watching. Fascination remains a
high point and is definitely worth seeking out; The Schoolgirl Hitchhikers is
also a worthy addition to any exploitation viewing list. I will file The Iron
Rose under odd films, although Francoise Pascal is certainly a highlight in
this gothic curiosity.
