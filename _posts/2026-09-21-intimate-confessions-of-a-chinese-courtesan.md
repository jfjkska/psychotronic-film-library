Intimate Confessions of a Chinese Courtesan (1972) is grindhouse meets
arthouse from the prolific Shaw Brothers. Directed by Chor Yuen and starring
Lily Ho as Ainu, who is kidnapped and trafficked to work in a brothel.

It is a bloody smorgasbord of nudity, kung fu and a strong female central
protagonist seeking "revenge through love, not hate".

Hailed as the first Chinese sex film, it was remade by the Shaw Brothers in
1984 as Lust for Love of a Chinese Courtesan. Recommended for fans of the
Shaw Brothers' extensive catalogue.
