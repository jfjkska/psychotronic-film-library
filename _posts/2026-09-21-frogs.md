Eco-exploitationer Frogs, directed by George McCowan in 1972, has a solid
cast, including Ray Milland playing the overbearing patriarch of a rich white
family. Sam Elliott, minus his trademark moustache, is the definitive photo
journalist come action man when nature fights back.

It should have been called Frogs, Lizards, Snakes, Birds etc, as all the
creatures of the swamp have a hand in the humans' downfall.

I have shared the trailer as it has a different death for Aunt Iris. The
original scene, of her lured to a swampy death by a giant butterfly, was cut
and replaced as it was seen to be silly.

The cinematographer is on point with the photography, the various reptiles
ramping up the menace as they become more predatory.

I leave you with this comic aside that appears after the closing credits fade
out: an animated frog hops onscreen with a human hand hanging out of its
mouth, after which it turns to face the audience and slurps the hand into its
mouth, then turns away from the audience and hops off screen. They really
don't make them like this anymore.
