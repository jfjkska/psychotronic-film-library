The Devil's Nightmare is a real gem. Erika Blanc plays a succubus who preys on
a group of stranded tourists staying overnight in a castle owned by a German
baron. Aided by his loyal staff, the Baron plays host to the tourists and,
unbeknownst to him, to the succubus.

The usual tropes expected in the best Eurosleaze horror are all present:
nudity, lesbian sex and bloodletting. Erika Blanc is suitably seductive even
when she goes full-on demon. She despatches the group one by one, but is
unable to seduce the young theologian, whose pact with the devil to save the
souls of the victims literally nosedives.

The soundtrack by Alessandro Alessandroni is suitably experimental, as you
expect from the Italian maestro, and is accompanied by "Julia", who provides
the ethereal vocals.

I have been aware of it for some time as it has come up on film threads. The
print on Tubi was near perfect, the dubbing was favourable and there were no
identifiable jarring edits, which can be an issue in these streamed versions
of Euro exploitation films. Definitely a big improvement on "James the
Orangutan" and Mary Woronov sleepwalking through Silent Night, Bloody Night.
This felt like a real diamond in the rough.

I wouldn't see it in a cinema though, as one character would only cause loud
laughter from parts of the audience that is not necessary. Go find it and let
me know what you think. It is definitely not a giallo, but it works within our
orbit of Euro cult film appreciation.
