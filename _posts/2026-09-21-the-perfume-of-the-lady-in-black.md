The Perfume of the Lady in Black is a very odd giallo from 1974, with a black
magic cult, insanity and cannibalism in the mix. Not a classic in any way, but
an interesting addition to the genre.

Francesco Barilli was the writer of Man from Deep River, but I don't think I
have come across him as a director before this.

I am noting it as of interest only, but wouldn't put it on a must-watch list.
