Black Candles, a.k.a. The Sexual Rites of the Devil, is a 1982 occult
exploitation film directed by José Ramón Larraz. Larraz directed the
English-set Vampyres, with its heady mix of sex and bloodletting, and this
follows a similar line: Carol arrives in England to visit her sister-in-law
after the death of her brother. What follows is a wild ride of debauchery,
sadism and farmyard sexual deviancy.

Although released in 1982, it has the cinema style of the 1970s. Larraz
reportedly disowned the film following its release. Best just to watch it and
make your own mind up, but watch out for the sabre scene, guaranteed to make
your eyes water.
