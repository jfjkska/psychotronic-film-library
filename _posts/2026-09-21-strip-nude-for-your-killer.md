With a title like Strip Nude for Your Killer (1975), you are going to expect
nudity. But it is as if director Andrea Bianchi had shouted "abbiamo bisogno
di piu nudita" every day on set.

One of the bloodiest gialli I have seen: fingers, ears and breasts all removed
by the killer in black leather motorcycle gear. The fact they wear a crash
helmet and use a car to despatch their victims is just another aside in the
plot. Critics called it "unabashedly sadistic and a more sordid example of the
giallo genre".

Starring the queen of the genre, Edwige Fenech, sporting a pixie haircut.
Fenech plays photography assistant to Nino Castelnuovo's Carlo, a womanising
chief photographer at the model agency where all the victims work. Carlo is
not above choking Magda when she questions whether he is the killer, but this
is seen as just part of their relationship for Magda. She is besotted and
ready to forgive him for his casual abuse.

A solid jazz score by Berto Pisano is one of the high points. Edwige plays
vulnerable Magda with compassion, but Castelnuovo's Carlo is played with
sleazy aplomb, which ultimately makes him unlikeable even when he is saving
Magda from threat.
