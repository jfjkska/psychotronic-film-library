Eyeball, aka Gatti rossi in un labirinto di vetro, or red cats in a glass
maze, is a 1975 film directed by exploitation kingpin Umberto Lenzi, infamous
for Nightmare City and Cannibal Ferox.

A Spanish/Italian production set among the tourist destinations of Barcelona.
Little is made of the city's landmarks, and Lenzi, who is known for his lack
of urgency, directs this pedestrian giallo with little flair or tension. Even
the usually reliable Bruno Nicolai struggles to get past a cocktail lounge
musical backdrop, with only brief flashes of his usual high-end scoring.

With a hard-boiled detective counting the days until he can go trout fishing,
it is his younger protege who is left to ask all the pertinent questions. "Are
you saying the killer is a sadist?" "I wouldn't rule it out," comes the reply.

With all this talk of trout fishing, Lenzi lays enough red herrings to fill a
fish market with misdirection and the usual highlighting of individuals as
possible suspects. The killer donning scarlet gloves and a red raincoat is
certainly unusual, and a penchant for removing the right eyeball of their
victims is certainly a novelty that somehow becomes apparent at the end, with
little of it making any sense.

One for the giallo completist.
