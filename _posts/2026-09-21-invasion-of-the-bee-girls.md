Denis Sanders' Invasion of the Bee Girls, from 1973, is a pretty good
production considering it is sci-fi nonsense.

An experimental doctor morphs women into sex-crazed queen bees who are wiping
out the straight men by heart failure due to sexual exhaustion. They just run
down the only gay man in the film with a car, as he doesn't succumb to their
advances.

Contains nudity and big hair. An exploitation favourite of mine.
