Tragic Ceremony is an occult slasher from 1972. It involves a black mass
slaughter, possession, revenge and possibly the Devil as the owner of a
petrol station.

Cipriani's score reaches pure melodrama from being barely noticeable. Camille
Keaton stars, a whole six years before her notorious lead in I Spit on Your
Grave. The bloodletting would give Fulci a run for his money, and Riccardo
Freda gets his money's worth by repeating the same head-slicing scene several
times in a flashback.

An interesting if uneven piece of Euro exploitation.
