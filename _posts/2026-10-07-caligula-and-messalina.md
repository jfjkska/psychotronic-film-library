Caligula and Messalina (1981) is Bruno Mattei's cheap cash-in on the
notoriety of the Bob Guccione-produced Caligula. Mattei pads his film
with sword-and-sandal stock footage around his story, where Messalina is
a gladiator set to win the attention of the unhinged Emperor.

Not as explicit as its more notorious namesake, it has elements of the
same incidents that are meant to be historical. The shortfalls in
production are evident throughout, and it becomes tiresome very quickly.
For Mattei completists, but best skip past this one for casual viewers.
