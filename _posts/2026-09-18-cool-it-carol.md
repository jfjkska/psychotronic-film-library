Pete Walker's sex comedy exploitationer (sic) from 1970 was based on a report
the director read in the now defunct Brit tabloid the News of the World. Carol
and Joe leave their small-town world to make it big in London. He thinks he can
get a job selling high-end cars; she wants to be a model after previously
winning a beauty contest in her home town. Reality soon hits and their meagre
cash pot is soon depleted. Carol turns to prostitution and, with Joe as her
pimp/manager, she negotiates sex work in escorting and porn films while finally
getting to model with her clothes on.

Janet Lynn is cute as Carol, who never seems to let her situation get on top of
her, and Robin Askwith plays it straight as Joe. Lynn didn't pursue a film
career, but Askwith hit pay dirt in Brit sex comedies under the Confessions
of... series. An exploitation curio for Pete Walker fans and those with an
interest in 1970s British films.
