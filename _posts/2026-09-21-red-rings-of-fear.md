Red Rings of Fear sits somewhere in the sphere of giallo but is more of a
police procedural, if the police is a cop who literally kidnaps a witness to
force him onto a rollercoaster to get information out of him. Fabio Testi is
such a cop, with two cats and a kleptomaniac girlfriend.

Elements of exploitation are not too far away, with frontal nudity from the
girls who attend the exclusive Catholic school, orgies involving penetration
by dildo, abortion and death by curling tongs.

Alberto Negrin directs with a degree of flair and doesn't linger on giving
time to unpick the plot. A great main title by soundtrack supremo Riz Ortolani
is used to maximum effect, and other elements of musique concrete and avant
jazz all contribute well.

Definitely one to check out for fans of Italian thrillers.
