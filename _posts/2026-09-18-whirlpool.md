Whirlpool, a.k.a. She Died with Her Boots On, is the 1970 debut from Spanish
auteur José Ramón Larraz. Larraz is notable for his bizarre plotting and
nudity, and this is no exception. Like his other films, Vampyres and Black
Candles, it is set in the English countryside.

The plot centres on the fruity goings-on of Aunt Sarah and nephew Theo as they
invite aspiring model Tulia to their home for a weekend to build on her photo
portfolio. What follows is a series of escalating sexual games involving strip
card games, sexual assault, voyeurism and threesome sex between Sarah, Theo and
Tulia. This is underpinned by a police investigation into a missing girl,
Rhonda, who had previously been a guest of the kinky aunt and nephew. Tulia
finds photos that show Rhonda to have been murdered during a photo shoot in the
woods, and is destined to suffer the same fate when Theo discovers her with the
incriminating pictures.

A truly international project: a Danish production, set in England, directed by
a Spaniard, with a great soundtrack by Italian genius Stelvio Cipriani.
