One of two macaroni cheese servings from Sergio Martino, the other being
[Hands of Steel]({{ '/reviews/hands-of-steel/' | relative_url }}).

2019: After the Fall of New York manages to combine elements of Mad Max, Escape
from New York and Planet of the Apes. The year is 2019, of course, 20 years
after the nuclear holocaust. Michael Sopkiw plays mercenary Parsifal, assigned
to bringing the only fertile woman back to Alaska, where the good guys are
going to repopulate the human race from space. Oliver Onions provide the Jan
Hammer-esque, Fairlight-heavy soundtrack that wheels through the film without
taking a break to take stock of the absurdity of the plot.

Both films are bookmarks in the history of Italian exploitation cinema, freely
borrowing not just from the big studio films but also from Castellari's Bronx
Warriors and The New Barbarians.
