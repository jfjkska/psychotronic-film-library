House on Straw Hill aka Exposé is a 1976 British exploitation gem written
and directed by James Kenelm Clarke. It stars genre heavyweights Udo Kier
and Linda Hayden. Due to part-financing by smut merchant Paul Raymond, his
girlfriend Fiona Richmond has a significant part as the girlfriend of
Kier's character.

Filmed on a balmy summer in Essex, England, Kier is a paranoid writer and
Hayden his secretary, hired to help finish his second novel. What you get
is a heady mix of sleaze and sex, and two thuggish youths who find
themselves at the wrong end of a smoking shotgun.

Kier plays the edgy, paranoid writer with his usual nervy flair, and
Hayden pouts, plots and manipulates. Richmond gets naked a lot and tries to
keep up with the proper actors. Kier said of Richmond that "she was a
famous person trying to be naked in a movie" — Richmond was better known
for recording an album of readers' letters from top-shelf magazine Men
Only than for her thespian skills, and it shows.

House on Straw Hill was cut for its cinema release and received an
outright ban in the UK when an uncut version turned up on video — landing
it on the Video Nasties list. In 2013, Severin Films released it on DVD
and Blu-ray, splicing two prints together to give the film its most
complete, uncut cut yet.
