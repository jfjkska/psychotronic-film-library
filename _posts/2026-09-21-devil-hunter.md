Jess Franco liked to film back to back, and Devil Hunter was subsequently done
so with Mondo Cannibale. I haven't seen the latter, but dived into Devil
Hunter with naive enthusiasm. According to Wikipedia it had been banned in the
UK, as it featured on the notorious video nasty list. Video waste of time
would be more appropriate. This must be the worst Franco film I have seen so
far.

A beautiful blonde model, Laura, arrives in South America and is subsequently
kidnapped and taken to an island to await a ransom payment. Little do the
kidnappers know, the island is frequented by a cannibalistic prowling naked
devil who is worshipped in fear by the local tribespeople. Vietnam vet Pete
and his pal, who is suffering with PTSD from his war experience, are
despatched to bring back the blonde model and the ransom money intact.

Much bloodshed is involved, a head becomes loose from a body, hearts are eaten
and Pete defeats the naked devil in a tussle on the cliff edge. The kidnappers
are dead, the local tribe rejoice, Pete and Laura escape with the ransom money
and all's well. Contains lots of nudity, banging on drums and some terrible
dubbed English accents.

Apparently it's better than Mondo Cannibale, so I will certainly be giving
that one a wide berth.
