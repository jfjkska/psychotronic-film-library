Devil in the Flesh, a.k.a. Venus in Furs (1969), is an Italian erotic drama by
Massimo Dallamano. I was hoping for Jess Franco's horror starring Klaus Kinski
but found this instead. Based on the book by Leopold von Sacher-Masoch, it
deals with the sado-masochistic relationship between Wanda and Severin. Severin
is aroused by pain and humiliation, and Wanda is a willing accomplice until she
grows tired of his role-playing and rejects Severin for casual stranger Bruno.
Bruno seeks control over the strange household and is soon brutalising
Severin, Wanda and the hired domestic staff.

The film faced censorship issues in its native Italy, and there is voyeurism,
nudity, sex and whippings in this heady psychodrama that would have been bold
choices in late-sixties Catholic Italy. Overall the performances by Laura
Antonelli and Régis Vallée are fine, but I didn't feel invested in the film and
will chalk it up as one more off the list. I need to source Franco's film, as
that was my original plan.
