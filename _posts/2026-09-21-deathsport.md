Deathsport (1978) is sometimes described as a sequel to Paul Bartel's satire
Death Race 2000. It certainly isn't that at all.

The film faced numerous problems, with rewrites, the director being physically
attacked by Carradine, and Jennings dealing with alcohol and cocaine
addiction. Not nearly as smart as Death Race 2000, but the explosions are
frequent and the action doesn't slow down.

Claudia Jennings' hair does not have a hair out of place despite wearing a
bike helmet for a lot of the film, and Carradine gives it his all. Jennings
died tragically in a head-on crash in 1979. Carradine described the film as
"terrible, my career never really recovered from that blunder".

Although not a classic, it ranks high in my theoretical post-apocalyptic
exploitation list and, as it is often said, they don't make films like this
anymore.
