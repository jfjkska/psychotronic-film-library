Queens of Evil is a 1970 Italian fantasy horror directed by Tonino Cervi.

A trio of sisters who are part of a cabal of satanic influencers seduce a
free-spirited biker who abandons his freedom and ultimately pays the price.
Big hair and groovy clothes set the scene for this dreamlike curio of "hippy
culture and sexual transgression".

It looks great, and its general weirdness marks this out as surreally
unique.
