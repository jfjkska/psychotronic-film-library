Vampyres (1975) is probably José Ramón Larraz's most well-known film. The
plot revolves around lovers Fran and Miriam, who were murdered and
resurrected as vampires. They survive by luring passing motorists to the
house where they were murdered and now inhabit.

The film hit censorship issues in the UK and faced severe cuts for gore
and sexual content. Larraz often filmed in the UK and is well placed in
his use of the British countryside for this sexy, bloody tale. One critic
remarked that "the film bares enough flesh and suggestive coupling to link
it to the softcore market."

The cast includes Sally Faulkner, a Shakespearean stage actress who was
not above slumming it in exploitation cinema. Her notable credits include
Norman J. Warren's Prey and Confessions of a Driving Instructor (both
1976), and Confessions from the David Galaxy Affair (1979).

If you like your vampire films brimming with sex and violence, then
Vampyres is the perfect blend of both.
