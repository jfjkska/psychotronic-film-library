British sex comedies get a harsh rap for their dismal entendres and poor
execution. Derek Ford's What's Up Nurse! (1977) is no exception to the rule.

A young doctor joins a seaside hospital where he is only the second doctor to
work there. There appear to have been cuts in the National Health Service from
as long ago as the mid 70s. He encounters a woman on a train who can only get
aroused when on a moving vehicle, an amorous landlady who wastes no time
undressing the doctor on her yacht, and a group of naked women playing ball
games in a muddy field. More nudity follows, including a stripping nurse who
is drugged with an aphrodisiac, and a woman with a chest complaint who goes
topless for the scene.

The humour is generally harmless but veers towards homophobia and racism, as
only British 70s comedy felt was necessary to get a cheap laugh. There is a
sequel, What's Up Superdoc!, so watch this space.
