Seven Deaths in the Cat's Eye is a 1973 period-set gothic giallo from
Antonio Margheriti. Jane Birkin plays an innocent convent girl returning to
the family pile in Scotland, where the big ginger cat is witness to several
murders.

I only counted six, seven with the death of James the large ape. You need to
watch it to get an understanding of why James, who is obviously a gorilla
but referred to as an orangutan, is even part of the plot.

Birkin gives it her best shot as the put-upon Corringa, and Serge Gainsbourg
is hilariously cast as a Scottish detective inspector investigating the
shady goings-on. Preposterous and completely absurd, but all the better for
it.

Superb soundtrack by Riz Ortolani, who uses strings, electronica and big
band jazz to great effect.
