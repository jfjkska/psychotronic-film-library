Emanuelle e Françoise (1975) is an interesting erotic thriller from Bruno Mattei
and Joe D'Amato. Emanuelle takes revenge on her sister Françoise's ex-boyfriend
Carlo, played by George Eastman. She locks him up in a padded cell with a two-way
mirror and injects him with LSD.

Meanwhile she performs a striptease, gets down with a car mechanic, and has a
threesome with Carlo's abandoned girlfriend and her blonde pal. Cue hallucinatory
cannibalism, death by meat cleaver, and someone accidentally trapped in the cell
with no escape. That basically sums it up.

Some jazz and lounge gems from Gianni Marchetti fill out the soundtrack to good
effect.
